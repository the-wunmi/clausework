SONGWRITER CO-PUBLISHING AND ROYALTY AGREEMENT

Composition: "Paper Lanterns"

This agreement is dated 1 December 2026 (the "Effective Date") and is between:

Tidewater Music Publishing LLC ("Publisher"), royalties@tidewatermusic.com;
Amara Osei ("Writer A"), amara.osei@fastmail.com;
Jonah Pell ("Writer B"), jonahpell.music@gmail.com; and
Lio Vance, producer ("Producer"), lio@vancesound.studio.

1. ADVANCE

1.1 Publisher shall pay Writer A an advance of USD 10,000 within ten (10) days of the Effective Date. The advance is recoupable only from Writer A's royalties under Section 2.

2. ROYALTIES

2.1 Publisher shall pay royalties quarterly, within forty-five (45) days after the end of each calendar quarter, on Net Receipts. "Net Receipts" means all sums Publisher receives from exploiting the Composition, less collection society fees.

2.2 Writer A receives twenty-five percent (25%) of Net Receipts, after Publisher has recouped the advance in Section 1.1 from Writer A's share.

2.3 Writer B receives fifteen percent (15%) of Net Receipts.

2.4 Producer receives ten percent (10%) of Net Receipts.

3. SYNCHRONIZATION FEES

3.1 For each synchronization licence of the Composition, Publisher shall pay Producer five percent (5%) of the licence fee within thirty (30) days of receiving it, in addition to Section 2.4.

4. STATEMENTS

4.1 Each payment comes with a statement showing Net Receipts for the quarter, the share applied, and any recoupment.

5. TERM

5.1 This agreement lasts for the full copyright term of the Composition.

Signed: Tidewater Music Publishing LLC; Amara Osei; Jonah Pell; Lio Vance
